## Chronology and objective

Inner fold year t=2021/22/23: train **2018 .. t−1** and validate year t; preprocessing/category encoder and market calibrator fit on prior history only (PR32 yearly `fold_calibrated_market`). Save all 6 checkpoints' race-macro Brier and log loss per year and actual row-OOF. **Each race has equal mass across inner years**: choose argmin over `sum_t (races_t * Brier_t) / sum_t races_t`, tie choose smaller round count. No peeking at either outer outcome for selection.
- Outer 2023: selected rounds from 2021+2022 inner only. Train 2018–2022 and predict 2023.
- Outer 2024: selected rounds from 2021+2022+2023 inner only. Train 2018–2023 and predict 2024.

For each learner and each outer year fit separate logit residual `alpha` with range [0,1.5], penalty `0.0001*(alpha−1)^2`, 2021+22 inner OOF for 2023 and 2021+22+23 inner OOF for 2024, using **only the 2023/2024 outer fold's independently prior-selected tree count** on the inner OOF. `P_scaled=sigmoid(logit(P_market)+alpha*(logit(P_model)-logit(P_market)))`. Include α=0 and α=1 identity unit tests; fit using SciPy bounded deterministic optimization and compare endpoint candidates. Never report in-sample alpha-fitting Brier as outer validation. Save both **raw and scaled** OOF.

## Strict reproducibility erratum — added after a failed original-model parity check, BEFORE scoring new candidate outer 2024

The first XGB outer 2023 baseline refit stopped at `max abs difference = 2.9796600298226394e-08` against the frozen 400-tree float32 forecast, before any candidate outer result was saved or compared. This value is within the precision of a float32 probability. A numerical 1e-8 absolute test was therefore overly strict for XGB's original stored probability representation. The model, hyperparameters, train years and labels are **unchanged**. Explicit acceptance is now `abs(new-ref) <= max(1e-8,2*spacing(float32(ref)))` for **every runner** AND global `max abs <= 1e-7`, with fail-closed diagnostics of the specific offending row. LightGBM/CatBoost remain on original strict 1e-8. All accepted maximum discrepancies and the effective criterion are written to the per-year manifest. This is a test-tolerance correction, **not a retuning of scores or data**.

Separately, the first LightGBM outer 2024 attempt stopped before model scoring because original PR32 DataFrame's year-filtered retained row indices differed from the newly sorted year-filtered indices. The fix sorts and resets both frames before comparing the complete composite key, binary outcomes and market probability; no row inclusion/exclusion changes. The previous completed inner curves and 2023 LightGBM outer predictions remain immutable checkpoints.

The original outer-year candidate results were NOT inspected to devise any new tree count or hyperparameter. The six checkpoints, nested-year choice, alpha procedure, CPU caps and 2024 B_raw selection gate remain exactly the original preregistered choices.
